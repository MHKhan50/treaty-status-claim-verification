How it works. The app reads the country, the treaty and what is claimed (signed, ratified or joined, withdrew, and whether it is negated) from your sentence, then looks up that country's year-by-year status. The decision is made by the record, not by a language model, so every verdict can be traced to a source. Paste a paragraph and each sentence that names a country and a treaty is checked separately.

Data. UNODA Treaties Database as published by Our World in Data (CC BY 4.0), downloaded live when this page opens. Statuses are yearly: Approved means ratified, accepted, approved or acceded. Check the UNODA Treaties Database for exact dates. Map: Natural Earth.

Limits. Four treaties only; yearly, not daily, status; sentences that do not name a country and one of the four treaties are not checked. Research tool, not legal advice.
